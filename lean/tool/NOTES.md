# lean/tool

A small local tool for the author: ship tuning charts and the flight sandbox in the browser.
It computes nothing itself: the server calls the game's own code (`lean.game.tuning`,
`lean.game.core`, `lean.sandbox.world.World`); the page only shows what the server returns.

```bash
uv sync --extra tools        # fastapi, uvicorn, websockets
PYTHONPATH=.:src uv run --no-sync python -m lean.tool [--port 8000]
```

Open http://127.0.0.1:8000/. The page loads nothing from the network: the charts are PNGs the
server draws with matplotlib (author 2026-11-24: matplotlib is the one charting system).

- **Ships tab.** A preset (the card's types), a time scale (1x real time, 1.5x, 2x the game,
  3x) and units, one of three systems (`lean/tool/units.py`, the one source of the factors:
  the server draws charts with it and sends it to the page): game (metric at the game's time
  scale), real metric (speeds, rates, Ps divided by the scale; m, m/s) and real imperial (mph, ft,
  ft/min for Ps). Turn rates are deg/s and loads g in all three. Sliders, readouts, both charts,
  their legends and notes and the sandbox HUD follow; the solve and the saved presets stay in
  game units.
  - **Inputs.** Every input of the tuning route, generated by the server from tuning's own
    lists (`design.inputs`): the handles `tuning.handles_of` returns, and every card field
    `tuning.handles_row` takes from its base row rather than from the handles (the game terms,
    the flaps, the stick levels, the nose; a field the card adds later appears without a
    change). Not offered: the half-lift drag (the card has the square polar only) and the zoom
    acceleration (it follows the dive's: one trade). Groups: performance targets, airframe,
    game terms, flaps, stick and nose, guns, shield/head-on/vision (38 inputs for the P-51D).
    `design.INFO` gives known inputs a label, unit, group and range (multiples of the preset's
    value, or physical bounds); an unknown one gets its name and 0.5-2x. Checkboxes: each term
    `tuning.POINT_MASS_TERMS` can turn off (drag rise, separation drag, the two soft limits,
    flap lift, flap drag; off sets its point-mass value), and "match" on the low-speed
    acceleration (off, the default: the preset's static thrust stays). Only moved inputs are
    sent; each change, 250 ms after the last, goes to the server, which solves the row through
    `tuning.handles_row` (fast mode). An unsolvable combination shows the solver's error.
  - **Charts.** The doghouse and the sustained turn (rate and radius against speed, the preset
    dotted), drawn by `docs/rewrite/sections/turns/doghouse.py` (`doghouse_figure`,
    `sustained_figure`), the same code as the committed PNGs: `POST /api/doghouse.png`,
    `POST /api/sustained.png`; the readouts from `POST /api/envelope` (corner, best and
    tightest sustained turn, top speed, best climb, stall speed, and the flown values once
    flown).
  - **Redraw** (2026-11-24, laptop CPU, 4 threads): about 0.7-0.8 s for a new design (the
    three requests; the solve about 0.25 s of it, up to 0.6 s for some inputs), 0.3 s for a
    units change.
- **Only what the game flies is drawn.** Each doghouse line (full, sustained and fine stick
  coasting; full stick diving and climbing) starts at the lowest speed at which a ship flown
  in the game actually flies it (`flown.achievable_speeds`): the game's flight tick
  (`core.flight_tick`, the flight of `core.step`), one ship, no fields, from the spawn state
  (spawn speed, spawn store), one of the 12 (stick, power) pairs held 0-30 s (a branch every
  0.5 s), then the condition held 4 s; the line is flown at a speed where the measured turn
  (the velocity's direction change per tick) is within 5% (and 0.2 deg/s) of the line's
  quasi-steady value at that speed and store, dive and climb only with the store inside the
  edge bands. The lowest states come from a climb (it fills the store and slows the ship to
  near its stall speed); then the nose must settle while the thrust accelerates the ship, so
  the large stick levels start higher. A random search (8192 ships, 60 s of random 0.2-12 s
  segments) found the same limits to 1.6 m/s on the flapped row (`flown.random_search`; the test runs a smaller one
  against the measurement it makes, so no stored numbers go stale).
  P-51D with the stick levels as fractions of the top line, flaps off (2026-11-24), game m/s
  at 2x (real: half): full stick 115.5, sustained stick 115.3, fine stick 103.4, dive 131.2
  (the store must be below 95%, which needs less total energy), climb 115.5; the stall speed
  is 90.4. The test's random search (1,024 ships, 40 s) now flies the sustained stick at
  111.4 m/s, 3.9 below this search's start, so its margin is 5 m/s (was 3). The Ps shading stops above the largest stick flown at each speed.
- **Flown checks.** `flown.critical_points` flies the corner (full stick from above the corner
  speed, the peak once the nose has settled), the best sustained turn (held by the stick that
  holds energy there, with a feedback on the speed) and the tightest sustained turn. The
  P-51D's tightest sustained turn lies where full stick gains energy (Ps > 0), just below the
  speed full stick settles at (120.6 m/s): coasting, the speed cannot be held there, so it is
  flown as a pass (the smallest radius passed through). P-51D, game units: corner 255.3 m/s 64.5 deg/s quasi-steady, flown 252.1 m/s 63.7 deg/s; best sustained 141.4 m/s
  37.98 deg/s, flown 141.4 m/s 37.98 deg/s (held; a fixed stick settles at 141.5 m/s);
  tightest sustained 120.3 m/s 202.1 m, flown 121.8 m/s 200.8 m (test: corner and tightest
  within 2%, best within 1%). Drawn as small hollow markers beside
  the quasi-steady points, with the flown numbers in the labels.
- **No flying in the slider path.** The flown checks take about 6 s (achievable speeds 5 s,
  points 1 s). The server flies the preset's once at start (in the background) and caches
  them; for a changed design the lines start at the preset's flown lowest speeds times the
  ratio of the stall speeds (labelled an estimate on the chart; within about 6% of the flown
  ones for g limit x1.3, stall speed x1.2, cruise x0.85, settled turn x1.2), until **Fly
  checks** (`POST /api/flown`) flies that design and its charts redraw with its own flown
  limits and points.
- **Sandbox tab.** Flies the design tuned in the Ships tab at its time scale against the
  scripted opponent; controls in `lean/sandbox/NOTES.md`. Each tab visit starts a new
  session: the server starts a child process that ticks the real `World` at 30 Hz and sends a
  frame per tick over `/api/sandbox`; the browser interpolates between frames.

The card is process-global (`tuning.flying`), which is why the sandbox runs in a child
process and why chart requests are serialised; `design.PRESET_ROWS` holds the presets as
imported, since `tuning.row_of` inside `flying` reads the design. Tests: `lean/tool/tests`.
