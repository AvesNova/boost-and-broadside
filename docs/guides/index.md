# Guides

Task-oriented pages. Each assumes the [installation](../getting-started/installation.md)
is done.

- [Training](training.md): run the training loop, choose a game, configure the pools and
  the mixed batch.
- [Queueing GPU jobs](queueing-jobs.md): the job queue and its runner, preemption of the
  champion's continuation, stopping and resuming runs gracefully.
- [Benchmarking](benchmarking.md): the benchmark lock, the cells, and how to measure a
  change.
- [Reference fixtures](reference-fixtures.md): what the goldens are, how the adapters and
  `REFERENCE_ARM` work, and why the fixtures are never edited.
- [Adding a mode](adding-a-mode.md): every place a new game mode plugs in.
- [Playing and spectating](playing.md): watch or fly a match, the views, the themes and
  the theme editor, offscreen renders.
- [Figures](figures.md): capture a policy's rollout and draw what its heads have
  learned, set by set.
