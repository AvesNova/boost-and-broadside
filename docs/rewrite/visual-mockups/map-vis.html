Example map with every visibility case

The larger example map (13 capsule fields) with the 16 cases of the perception.py rules placed on it. Green-side view; vision 290 m per ally, team-shared. Edge bands are 20 m per level (40 m at n = 0.5 or 2, as in the current code's 40 m transition), contours every third of a level, bold line = core edge, which blocks sight. Mint thin air, pink thick air, navy / amber ships, fog at 60%. Dashed outlines are hidden enemies, shown only for checking.

  1. Visible: in range of A1, clear line of sight.
  2. Hidden: out of range of every ally.
  3. Hidden: in range of A1, but behind the big cloud's core (sight shadow).
  4. Hidden: inside the central cloud's core; no ally is inside it.
  5. Visible: inside the top jetstream's core, together with ally A2.
  6. Visible: in the big cloud's see-through edge band, outside its core.
  7. Hidden: where the bottom-left jetstream overlaps a cloud; A3 is inside the jetstream only, so the cloud's core blocks.
  8. Visible: seen only by A4 (out of A1's range); vision is shared.
  9. Revealed: beyond A1's range, but fired this tick.
  10. Revealed: just spawned at its base.
  11. Hidden: right next to A2, but A2 is inside the jetstream's core and cannot see out.
  12. Own bullets (A1 firing at 3): always visible, even through the shadow.
  13. Enemy bullets (from 9): visible where A1 could see them; the faint one near the shooter is out of range.
  14. Ally respawning: timer ring at the base.
  15. Field states: the top jetstream and the bottom-left jetstream are seen into (allies inside); every other core is opaque under the fog.
  16. Terrain is always known: arena edge, fields, bases and turrets are drawn under fog.
